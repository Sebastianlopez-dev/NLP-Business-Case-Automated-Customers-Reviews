Natural Imbalance

213.9K

We processed 33 categories. However, the data revealed an 81.6% positive skew. The minority class dictated the final balanced corpus size. We discarded ~728K positive reviews.

Word Count Limit

98.5%
fit in 128 tokens

Median length is 27 words. Truncating at max_length=128 yields a 4× training speedup with negligible signal loss (<1.5%).

Data Quality

8.4%
duplicates

81,901 duplicated wildcards (e.g. "Great", "Good"). This strict noise filter prevents model overfitting on low-signal repeats.

Lexical Extremes

31
vs 14,735 words

Negatives use a compact vocabulary of 31 words (waste, broke). Positives use a rich lexicon of 14,735 variations. Praise is diverse; complaints are repetitive.

Platform Sociology

  • Helpful Votes: 1★ reviews get 2.5× more votes. Users engage with alerts.

  • Unverified: Spike heavily at 4★. A common tactic to inflate ratings covertly.
