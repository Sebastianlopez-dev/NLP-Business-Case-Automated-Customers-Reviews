Natural Imbalance

213.9K

We processed 33 categories. However, the data revealed an 81.6% positive skew. The minority class dictated the final balanced corpus size. We discarded ~728K positive reviews.

Word Count Limit

98.5%
fit in 128 tokens

Median length is 27 words. Truncating at max_length=128 yields a 4× training speedup with negligible signal loss (<1.5%).

Data Quality

8.4%
duplicates

81,901 duplicated wildcards (e.g. "Great", "Good"). This strict noise filter prevents model overfitting on low-signal repeats.

Clustering Strategy

HybridKMeans Pipeline

Two algorithms, one result. K-Means (Lloyd) computes mathematically clean centroids at full convergence. MiniBatchKMeans scales to the full 571M-review dataset at O(batch·k·d) memory. 6 clusters selected via elbow method.

nomic-embed-text-v1.5

Projects 219,998 reviews into 768 dimensions. Replaced MiniLM with a 10-point MTEB improvement. Better embeddings → better clusters.

k=6 via Elbow + Domain Logic

Silhouette 0.0311 (expected in 768-dim space). k=8 split hairs; k=4 lumped unrelated categories. 6 clusters map cleanly to real product domains.

Why HybridKMeans

Two algorithms, one result. K-Means (Lloyd) provides mathematically clean centroids; MiniBatchKMeans scales to 571M reviews at O(batch·k·d) memory.

Centroids from K-Means — n_init=10, max_iter=300, converged in 112 iterations (57.1s). Silhouette: 0.0304.

Labels from MiniBatchKMeans — batch_size=1,024, 10 epochs, 3 inits, best kept. Fit in 19.6s. Silhouette: 0.0311.

Blog Generation

Persona: Senior market analyst.
Input: Pre-extracted facts per cluster: review counts, sentiment stats, top/worst product ASINs, review excerpts, TF-IDF terms.
Output: 600–900 word blog article with 5 sections.
Cost: ~700 tokens per cluster × 6 = ~4,300 total per run.

Playground Personas

Positive → Customer Success: celebrate the win, ask about daily use.
Neutral → Product Specialist: name the product, ask a technical detail.
Negative → Support Lead: identify root issue, propose concrete next step.

Architecture Principle

Extractive-Abstractive split. Python (pandas) extracts hard facts deterministically. Gemini only handles styling — transforming structured data into narrative. The LLM never touches raw reviews. Every product ID and percentage in the output is traceable to the extractive phase.
